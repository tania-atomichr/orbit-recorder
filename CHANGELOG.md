## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Record a call on Google Meet, Zoom or Teams in the browser: a card offers
  it, one press records both sides, a bar with levels, Pause and Stop,
  a consent reminder, warnings when nothing is heard or the microphone is
  off, notes typed during the call, and optional live words (Deepgram).
- Record a video of a tab, window, screen or part of the screen, with your
  face in a round bubble, a recording strip with a live preview, and the
  last setup in one press.
- Record audio: your voice, or your voice and the computer's sound.
- Make a step-by-step guide by clicking through a task: sentences for
  clicks, fields, dropdowns, shortcuts, double and right clicks, going back,
  drags and tabs; clips for steps that move; typing never kept; password and
  card fields painted out.
- Guide editor: write it for me, reorder, add a step, tips, select and
  delete, Undo, and a picture editor (cover, redraw the ring, no ring).
- Note page: player, trim and clip a moment, write-ups from templates
  (your own too), summary with short, in depth and Q&A, next steps, who
  talked, mood, ideas, chapters, ask about the recording, transcript with
  find, names and translation, and an editor for your own notes.
- Library: find anything said, filter, select many, upload a recording.
- Services with your own keys: Deepgram, AssemblyAI, OpenAI Whisper or your
  own for transcripts; Claude, OpenAI-compatible or your own for write-ups.
- Places to send to: a signed webhook (with the file if you want), Notion,
  Google Docs, Teamtailor and HubSpot, with retries and "Sent" states.
- `orbit-note/1` and `step-guide/1` formats, `<orbit-note>` and
  `<orbit-guide>` embed, and an example receiver.
