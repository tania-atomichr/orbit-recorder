Orbit Recorder is ready

  1. Pin it

    Click the puzzle piece at the top right of Chrome, then the pin beside Orbit Recorder, so its icon stays in the toolbar.

  2. Allow the microphone once

    So your side is recorded when you start from the toolbar.

  3. Allow the camera once

    If you want your face in the corner of a video. Its name then shows under Camera in Record a video.

  4. Choose your services

    Transcripts and write-ups use your own keys (Deepgram, AssemblyAI, OpenAI, Claude, or your own). Then connect where notes go: your app, Notion, Google Docs, Teamtailor, HubSpot.

    Open Settings

Then

  1. On a call

    Meet, Zoom or Teams in Chrome: press Record on the card at the top of the call, or the toolbar icon (Option Shift R). Press it again, or Stop on the bar, to stop.

  2. Anywhere else

    Press the icon for the menu: Record a video of a tab, a window, part of the screen or the whole screen; Record audio; or Make a step-by-step guide. Typing and passwords are never recorded in a guide.
