The headings a write-up has, and a line on what goes under each. Built-in ones: Meeting, Sales or discovery call, Interview, One-to-one, Customer support call, Video explainer, Voice note.

Where things go

Send finished notes and guides to your own app, Notion, Google Docs, Teamtailor or HubSpot. Zapier, Make and n8n work through a webhook. Each place can take everything by itself, or only what you send it by hand.
